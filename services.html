What We Treat

Expert Gynecological Services

Women encounter a range of gynecological and infertility issues, often impacting their overall health, reproductive capabilities, and quality of life. Dr. Anjani provides compassionate, evidence-based care across all these conditions.

🩸 Menstrual Disorders

Irregular, painful, or heavy periods can significantly affect daily life. Dr. Anjani diagnoses the root cause — whether hormonal imbalances, uterine abnormalities, or structural issues — and provides targeted, effective treatment to restore normal menstrual health.

🔵 Fibroids

Uterine fibroids are benign tumours that can cause pain, heavy bleeding, and fertility issues. Dr. Anjani offers both medical management and minimally invasive surgical options, helping patients avoid unnecessary hysterectomy wherever possible.

🔴 Endometriosis

Endometriosis — tissue growing outside the uterus — causes severe pain, irregular cycles, and infertility. Dr. Anjani specialises in advanced laparoscopic excision surgery and long-term management strategies to improve quality of life.

🦠 Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)

PID is an infection of the reproductive organs that can lead to chronic pelvic pain and long-term infertility risk. Early diagnosis and targeted antibiotic treatment, along with follow-up care, prevent complications and protect future fertility.

🟡 Ovarian Cysts

Fluid-filled sacs on the ovaries can cause pain, bloating, and hormonal disruption. Dr. Anjani provides accurate diagnosis through ultrasound and offers medical or laparoscopic surgical management depending on the type and size of the cyst.

⚙️ PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome affects menstruation, hormone levels, weight, and fertility. Dr. Anjani takes a holistic approach combining lifestyle interventions, medication, and fertility support to manage PCOS and its long-term health implications.

🔬 Cervical & Uterine Conditions

Polyps, abnormal Pap smears, cervical erosion, and structural uterine abnormalities require careful evaluation and treatment. Dr. Anjani provides comprehensive management of these conditions, including colposcopy and hysteroscopic procedures.

🤱 Infertility Treatment

A thorough diagnostic work-up of both partners forms the basis of infertility management. Dr. Anjani offers personalised treatment plans including ovulation induction, IUI, and coordination of IVF cycles with specialist IVF centres.

🏥 Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive keyhole surgery for a range of gynecological conditions — fibroids, endometriosis, ovarian cysts, ectopic pregnancy, and adhesions. Patients benefit from smaller incisions, less pain, and faster return to normal life.

🏨 Hysterectomy

When necessary, Dr. Anjani performs hysterectomy using the least invasive approach suitable for each patient — laparoscopic, vaginal, or abdominal. A thorough discussion of all alternatives is always conducted before surgery is recommended.

🤰 Obstetric Care

Dr. Anjani provides comprehensive antenatal care, monitoring mother and baby through all trimesters, managing high-risk pregnancies, and ensuring safe delivery. Postnatal care and guidance is also provided after birth.

✨ Cosmetic Gynecology

Personalised intimate wellness treatments to restore comfort, confidence, and function. Services include vaginal rejuvenation, labiaplasty, hymenoplasty, postpartum restoration, and PRP rejuvenation. Learn more →
