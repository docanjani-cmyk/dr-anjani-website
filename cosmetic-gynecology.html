Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation completely confidential?

Yes, absolutely. All consultations and procedures are conducted with the strictest confidentiality. Dr. Anjani maintains complete privacy for all patients seeking cosmetic gynecology services.

How do I know if I need a cosmetic gynecology procedure?

The best approach is to book an initial consultation. Dr. Anjani will discuss your concerns, conduct an examination if appropriate, and provide honest guidance on whether a procedure is likely to benefit you — she will never recommend unnecessary treatments.

What is the recovery time for these procedures?

Recovery time varies depending on the specific procedure. Minimally invasive treatments like PRP typically have minimal downtime. Surgical procedures like labiaplasty may require 1–2 weeks of recovery. Dr. Anjani will provide detailed post-procedure care instructions.

Are these procedures covered by insurance?

Cosmetic gynecology procedures are generally not covered by standard health insurance. However, Dr. Anjani offers transparent, affordable pricing and can discuss payment options during your consultation.
