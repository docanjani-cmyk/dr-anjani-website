Dr. Anjani Dixit

Dr. Anjani is an experienced advanced laparoscopic surgeon and fertility expert with over 12 years of dedicated practice. She combines advanced surgical skills with a compassionate, personalised approach to care, ensuring that patients are fully heard and supported throughout their treatment journey.

Education & Training

Dr. Anjani completed her medical education at IPGME&R and SSKM Hospital, Kolkata — one of India's premier medical institutions. She went on to complete her residency at Holy Family Hospital, New Delhi, gaining extensive clinical experience across a wide range of gynecological conditions.

She subsequently pursued fellowship training in minimal access surgery and reproductive medicine, equipping her with the latest advanced surgical techniques and fertility management protocols.

Personal

Dr. Anjani is a mother of two daughters and understands first-hand the physical and emotional journey that women go through. Outside the clinic, she enjoys swimming, reading, and storytelling. This personal perspective deeply informs her empathetic, patient-centred approach to care.

Approach to Care

Dr. Anjani believes in balanced treatment – avoiding unnecessary interventions while ensuring timely and appropriate care. She creates a safe, judgment-free environment where patients feel comfortable discussing even the most sensitive concerns. She considers the whole person, including their mental, emotional, and social circumstances, not just the medical diagnosis.
