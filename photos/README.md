# Photo archive

Originals as they arrive, by month. **Not** under `public/`, and that is the
point: everything in `public/` is served by URL once deployed, and these
originals include photos of patients and their families.

A photo only becomes public when it is deliberately copied into
`public/Gallery/` with a descriptive filename and added to `app/lib/gallery.js`.
Check every one at full resolution first — whiteboards, monitors, wristbands and
notes in the background have carried a patient's name before.
